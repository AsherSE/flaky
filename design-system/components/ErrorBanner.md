# ErrorBanner

A soft red block at the top of the card for anything that went wrong.

- `error-bg` fill, `error-ink` text, `radius-lg`, 12px padding, text-sm.
- Say what to do next, plainly: "Wrong code", "Add your name to continue, or tap Skip for now.", "Verification failed. Try again."
- One at a time, above the form; clear it on the next attempt.

_Implemented inline in `src/app/page.tsx`._
