# TimeOfDayToggle

Three round icon radios (sunrise, sun, moon) that tag a plan as Morning, Lunch or Night; optional, and tapping the selected one clears it.

- Idle: `line-input` ring, `muted` icon; hover goes `terracotta`. Selected: solid `terracotta`, white icon.
- Sits to the right of the date field on the same underline row.
- 36px targets (`tap-36`), 20px stroke icons at 1.75 weight. The icons are also under Icons.

_Implemented inline in `src/app/page.tsx`._
